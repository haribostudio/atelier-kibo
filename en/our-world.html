01 — The manifesto

Moments that stay

“Last summer's salt still clinging to the chain” ✳︎

We pass through seasons, cities, nights, stories. Some go by quickly; others stay with us for a long time. KIBO is there for all these moments — the ones we were waiting for, the ones we hadn't planned, the ones we'd love to live again.

Our jewelry is worn over time — mixed, scratched, changed along with us. It keeps a little of every place, every encounter, every good energy we want to carry with us.

Pieces made to live with you, and to carry through everything that comes after.

02 — The five pillars

What guides us

I Sun Not a season: a state of mind.Chasing the light, even when summer is far away.
II Sea The sea is movement.Impromptu departures, returns that feel different.
III Sound Music accompanies the moments we end up never forgetting.
IV Energy The connection between people.Some energies can't quite be explained. We just know we want to keep them.
V Memory What we keep, even when the moment has passed.
03 — The materials

What we place in your hands

Each bead is chosen for what it is: its shape, its shine, its character. Quality materials, carefully sourced from suppliers we know — then assembled by hand, one by one, at the atelier in Paris.

Freshwater pearls The heart of the pieces

Baroque, irregular, alive. No two are identical — which is exactly what we love.

Glass beads The color

Spun glass, millefiori, smileys, flowers and stars — the touches that give each piece its mood.

Mother-of-pearl The softness

Pastel reflections that change with the light, from morning to night.

Steel & aged silver What lasts

Stainless steel chains and clasps, aged silver details. Designed for water, salt and time.
