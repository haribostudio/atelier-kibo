L'Atelier

A piece that doesn't exist yet: yours. A memory, a color, a name, a beach — we turn it into a necklace or bracelet, designed with you and assembled by hand at the atelier, in Paris.

1 — The conversation

Tell us what you have in mind: the occasion, the colors, the materials, the budget. In writing first — then by appointment at the atelier if we live in the same city, or by video call.

2 — The design

We propose a composition: spun-glass beads, freshwater pearls, charms. Nothing starts without your approval — and a firm price.

3 — The making

The piece is assembled by hand, one of a kind. Allow two to three weeks, delivery included.

Tell us

Thank you — this is the beginning of something. We'll write to you within 24 to 48 h with the next steps.

“Every made-to-order piece begins with a story.” ✳︎
